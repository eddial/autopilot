## Routing
Customers and deals: sales questions, customer issues and support
requests, security questionnaires, contracts and renewals. Investor and
board mail stays in company.
Urgent: a customer outage, or a deadline within 24 hours.

## Work
Drafts go in the customer's language and reply in the original thread.
Security answers come from the trust center doc first; mark anything
it does not cover as `[check]` instead of guessing. For a bug report,
reproduce it if you can and say in the briefing what you found.
