Description
Turns purchase orders from email into draft sales orders in Exact Online.
Instructions
1. Read the order from the email or its attachment.
2. Find the customer in Exact by name or VAT number.
3. Match each line to an article and check the price.
4. Create the sales order as a draft for review.
