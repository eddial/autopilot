Interface sounds for soundtrack.py --sfx.
- click-1 … click-6.wav: six isolated clicks from "Clicking on Laptop Trackpad" by justamudkip (freesound.org/s/854747), CC0.
- type.wav: "Apple Mac Keyboad typing close up" by khenshom (freesound.org/s/563505), CC0. Trimmed to 14 s.
- voice.wav: a voice on a call, made from a macOS system voice (Samantha, `say`) cut into reversed syllables and
  band-passed like a phone line, so no words survive. Made for this toolkit; no outside source.
Trimmed, faded and normalised; nothing else changed.
