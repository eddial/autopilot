Lleverage Feature Drop Week #46 🎁

Big week for pages. Describe the page you want and the Agent builds it next to you, data connected and every widget checked.

Every order desk looks at its work differently. One team wants orders waiting on a PO, the next wants late deliveries per supplier, the one after that a heat map nobody has a chart for. A standard dashboard never fits all of them, so each company ends up with its own system. That only works if building it is as quick as asking for it.

So now the Agent draws widgets of its own when a chart won't do, and you can still move things around by hand. Working with it got more direct too. Point at an order on screen and ask why it's on hold. Paste a supplier's confirmation straight into the chat. New requests can land in your team's Teams channel.

My favourite: pointing. Clicking the row beats describing it every single time.
