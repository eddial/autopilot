Lleverage Feature Drop Week #46 🎁

The more of your operations run on their own, the more you need to see what they're doing.

When agents enter the orders and match the invoices, nobody watches them come in anymore. You need a page that shows you: orders waiting on a PO, late deliveries per supplier, what got stuck and why. Every order desk wants a different one, and a standard dashboard never fits.

So this week is about pages. Describe the page you want and the Agent builds it next to you, data connected and every widget checked. When a chart won't do, it draws a widget of its own, like a heat map of late orders per customer and weekday. And you can still move things around by hand.

Working with the Agent got more direct too. Point at an order on screen and ask why it's on hold. Paste a supplier's confirmation straight into the chat. New requests can land in your team's Teams channel.

We're not done cooking. Pages will soon get interactive widgets that run as small apps, so you can act on what you see without leaving the page. More to come.
