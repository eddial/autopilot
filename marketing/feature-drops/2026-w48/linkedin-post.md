Lleverage Feature Drop Week #48 🎁

Good week: pages in Lleverage now work like a document. You type straight onto the page, drop a table or a chart between the lines, and ask the Agent to build a new page for you.

In an autonomous back office the agents handle the orders and invoices that can run on their own, and people step in for the decisions. Every company runs that work its own way, so the people who run it have to be able to shape the system themselves, and correct it the moment something's off.

So this week is about doing it yourself. Change a widget in a form instead of asking for it. Put your orders on a timeline and drag one to another day. Edit your message while the Agent is still working, or point at a spot in a report it made and say what to change there. Schedules only run when there's something new. And more.

My favourite: the timeline. A week of orders per customer, in one look.
