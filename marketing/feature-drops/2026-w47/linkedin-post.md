Lleverage Feature Drop Week #47 🎁

Big week: Lleverage has a new navigation. Your chats and everything waiting on you now sit in one list, and builders reach every page they work on from one place.

In an autonomous back office the agents handle the orders and invoices that can run on their own, and people step in for the decisions. That only works when those decisions are easy to find and easy to trust. Every company runs its back office its own way, so the system around it has to be built for that company, and checked by the people who run it.

So this week is also about seeing what's behind the work. Click a number on a page and you get the orders behind it. An approval lists exactly what the Agent will send to your ERP before you say yes. And you can steer the Agent while it works.

My favourite: copying a workflow to another project. Build and test it in one, run it in the other.
