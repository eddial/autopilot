Lleverage Feature Drop Week #43 🎁

This week the Agent learned to work with the rest of your team, not just with you.

The back office doesn't run on one person. A discount needs a manager's okay, an exception needs the colleague who knows the customer, and how things are really done often lives in someone's head. So we taught the Agent to bring those people in.

What that looks like this week:
→ Walk the Agent through a process on a screen call, in your own ERP. It asks as you go, writes each step down, and builds a skill from the notes when you ask.
→ Before it runs a workflow that needs approval, or deletes one that's still in use, it asks you first and shows what would break.
→ The composer suggests your next message, and Tab finishes what you started typing.
→ Request forms work out totals and discounts while people fill them in.

My favourite: the Agent can hand part of a job to a colleague. Mila approves the discount right on the card, or works through it with her own Agent, and your session picks up where it left off.
