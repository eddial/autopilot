Lleverage Feature Drop Week #43 🎁

Good week. The Agent got a lot better at working with people, not just for them.

We're building towards an autonomous back office: orders, invoices and the rest handled by agents, with people stepping in where their judgement matters. That only works if the Agent learns how your company actually does things, and knows when to pull someone in.

So now you can walk the Agent through a process on a screen call, in your own ERP, and it writes down every step, ready to become a skill. It asks before it runs a workflow that needs approval, or deletes one that's still in use. The composer suggests your next message, request forms do the maths for the person filling them in, and a skill can be copied into another project. And more.

My favourite: the Agent can hand a task to a colleague. Mila approves the discount, or works it through with her own Agent, and your session carries on with her answer.
