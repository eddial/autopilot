Lleverage Feature Drop Week #43 🎁

An order comes in from Van Dijk Metaal asking for 8% off. The order desk can only give 5%.

Until now, that's where the Agent stopped and someone had to go chase a manager. This week it just asks Mila. She gets a task, approves it on the card or talks it through with her own Agent, and the Agent drafts the order in the ERP. Nobody forwards an email.

That's the thread through this week: a back office runs on more than one person, so the Agent now knows how to bring people in. You can also show it how you do something on a screen call, and it takes notes it can turn into a skill. It checks with you before it runs or deletes anything that needs approval. And request forms do their own maths now.
